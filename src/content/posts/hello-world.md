## Why now?

I've been a software engineer for several years, and like most developers I've read countless blog posts, saved endless bookmarks, and learned from people generous enough to share what they know. At some point you realise you should probably give something back.

So here we are.

## What this blog is about

This isn't going to be a purely technical blog. Some posts will go deep on code — architecture decisions, language quirks, tooling I love (or hate). Others will be more personal — reflections on the craft, career lessons, and whatever I'm thinking about outside of work.

The rough split I'm aiming for:

- **Tech** — Vue, TypeScript, databases, system design, tooling
- **Process** — how I work, how I think about problems, how I've grown as an engineer
- **Personal** — sport, reading, anything that keeps me sane

## What I'm hoping to get out of it

Writing is thinking. It forces you to be precise in a way that thinking alone doesn't. I expect a lot of these posts will be me working through something I haven't fully figured out yet.

If it's useful to someone else along the way — brilliant.

## How often will I post?

Honestly? I don't know. I'm not going to commit to a schedule and then burn out in month two. I'll write when I have something worth saying.
